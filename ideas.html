Tasting & Reviews 10 ideas

  • First Pour

    “I’ve never tasted this before. Honest first sip, no notes.”

    Reel 45–60s + YT 8–12 min — Unscripted reaction, then nose/palate/finish, then verdict.

  • Under-$40 Shelf Hunt

    “$40 at a Maryland liquor store. What’s actually worth it?”

    Reel 60–90s — Walk the aisle, grab 3, taste at home. Strong save/share bait.

  • Buy / Bar Pour / Pass

    “Would I spend my own money on this again?”

    Reel 30–45s (series) — Your signature 3-tier verdict. Same graphic every time.

  • Bottled-in-Bond Night

    “This label has a law from 1897 behind it.”

    Reel + carousel — Taste a BiB, explain the 4 rules on screen.

  • Drop-by-Drop

    “Watch what 3 drops of water do to a cask strength.”

    Reel 45s — Close-up macro of the swirl; notes change on screen.

  • Neat vs. Old Fashioned

    “Great neat. But does it survive a cocktail?”

    Reel 60s — Your bartender edge. Builds credibility no other beginner has.

  • First Peat (unscripted)

    “My first Islay scotch. Not rehearsed.”

    Reel 30s + YT — Do not re-take. The real face is the content.

  • Store Pick vs. Standard

    “Same distillery, one barrel picked by a store. Worth $20 more?”

    YT 10 min + Short — Blind both, reveal at end.

  • Sample Tuesday

    “A brand sent me this. Here’s what I really think.”

    Reel 45s — Always disclose (#ad / gifted). Honesty builds trust.

  • Monthly Top 3

    “Of 15 whiskeys this month, these 3 stood out.”

    Carousel + Short — Pulls from journal; pin to profile.

Learning Journey POV 10 ideas

  • Day 1

    “I’m a bartender who wants to become a whiskey expert. Come with me.”

    Reel 60s + YT intro — Mission statement and channel trailer. Pin it.

  • Study Hall

    “Week 3 of WSET Level 2. Here’s what clicked.”

    Weekly Reel/Short — Desk POV, highlighter, one key lesson per week.

  • What I Got Wrong

    “3 things I believed about whiskey that were wrong.”

    Reel 45s (monthly) — Vulnerability = engagement. Easy comments.

  • Aroma Kit Blind

    “Training my nose. Can I name these jars blind?”

    Reel 30–45s — Score yourself on screen. Repeat monthly to show progress.

  • Then vs. Now

    “My notes on this bottle 90 days ago vs. today.”

    Reel + YT — Split-screen of old/new journal notes.

  • Book to Glass

    “Read about mash bills, so I tasted 3 to prove it.”

    Reel 60s — Turns study into a tasting.

  • Journal Flip

    “My tasting journal, page by page.”

    Carousel (photos) — Your photography shines here. Overhead, natural light.

  • Behind the Bar

    “Questions guests ask me about whiskey every shift.”

    Reel 30s — Shot at Kache after close. Answer one per episode.

  • Whiskey Counter

    “Whiskey #27 of 100.”

    On-screen tag on every review — A visible goal keeps people following the arc.

  • Exam Day

    “I just sat the WSET Level 2 exam.”

    Reel + YT vlog — Before/after, then results reaction later.

Head-to-Head 10 ideas

    Reel 60s — Isolates the mash bill.

  • Scotch vs. Japanese

    “Same price, two countries. Blind.”

    Reel 60s + YT — Great for the Japanese-month tentpole.

  • Wheated vs. High-Rye

    “Soft vs. spicy bourbon.”

  • Pot Still vs. Blend (Irish)

  • ASM vs. Speyside

    Reel 60s + YT — Category-builder content.

  • Your Pick vs. Mine

    Reel (monthly) — Use Story polls to pick. Drives community.

  • Bartender vs. Regular

    “My bar regular vs. me. Blind.”

    Reel 60s — Get signed release; adds personality.

  • Peat Ladder

    “From lightly smoky to campfire, ranked.”

    Reel 45s — Bracket graphic.

Education & How-To 10 ideas

    “What every word on a bourbon label actually means.”

    Reel 60s + carousel — Straight, BiB, single barrel, “small batch” (no legal definition).

  • Bourbon vs. Rye vs. Tennessee

    Reel 60s + YT — Side-by-side rule graphic.

  • Scotch Regions Map

    Reel 30s — Pairs with Drop-by-Drop.

  • Glassware Test

    “Same whiskey, three glasses, three experiences.”

    Reel 45s — Glencairn vs. rocks vs. copita.

  • Mash Bill 101

    “Corn, rye, wheat, barley. What each one tastes like.”

    Reel 60s — Raw grain props on the bar top.

  • Age Statements

  • Pot vs. Column Still

    “Two machines, very different whiskeys.”

  • 5-Bottle Home Bar

    “Build a whiskey shelf that covers everything.”

    YT 10 min + carousel — Evergreen SEO; update every year.
